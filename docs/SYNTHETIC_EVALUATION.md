Seeds 17/18 were already used during development; additional seeds from the same generator are not an external holdout.

Ground truth comes from generated masks, applied blur and geometric translations. The detector receives pixels without scenario labels. Sequence-level TP/FP/TN/FN use whether any confirmed opposing track appears in the sequence. Consecutive frames and within-page pairs are dependent; they are not independent samples for a confidence interval. There was no training, threshold tuning after results or arbitrary accuracy pass threshold.
